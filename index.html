You pack what people leave behind.

The recently deceased arrive with six or seven belongings and a handful of peculiar packing rules. Fit everything into the suitcase, then seal it for Final Transit.

  1. Select a belonging, then click roughly where you want it. Large items automatically snap inside the suitcase edges.
  2. Rotate long pieces when the case allows it. Click a placed piece to select it again.
  3. Watch the packing notes: green means the rule currently passes. Weight is just case-file flavour in this prototype, not a puzzle rule.
  4. Some arrangements reveal a story temptation. You may knowingly seal that exception — accidents are just paperwork.

Case 1 is the tutorial. Start with the reading glasses and make them share a side with the letters. Diagonals do not count.
